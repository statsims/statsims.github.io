Gondola Kinematics

Arm → mount → auto-records on release → analyse & export. Needs HTTPS.

Procedure
  1. Open the page via its https:// link directly in Safari or Chrome (not a file, not an in-app preview).
  2. Set mass (phone + gondola) and wire length if known.
  3. Tap Arm. During the countdown, fix the phone rigidly to the gondola, screen on.
  4. Keep it still for calibration (beep). Two beeps = armed.
  5. Release. Recording starts automatically when acceleration exceeds the trigger.
  6. At the bottom, tap Stop before handling the phone, or trim the handling out afterwards.
  7. Check the trim markers, then export.

Setup

The browser caps the real rate, often 60 Hz on Android and ~100 Hz on iOS. The measured rate is shown and logged.
